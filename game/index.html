The Blindfold Coin Puzzle

Dad says nobody gets this on the first try. Prove him wrong! 🙈
The rules
  1. There are 100 coins. Exactly 10 are heads up, the rest are tails.
  2. You're blindfolded. You can't tell which coins are heads.
  3. Split the coins into two piles. Any sizes you like.
  4. You get one move: flip every coin in one pile.
  5. Blindfold off! You win if both piles have the same number of heads.
