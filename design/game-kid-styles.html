1. What makes the current page hard for kids

TodayProblem for a kidFix (goes into whichever style you pick)
5 lines of written rulesToo much reading; pre-readers can't play at all3 picture cards + a 🔊 Read it to me button (the browser's built-in voice, free)
100 tiny 30px coins, tap one at a timeSmall targets, and 10+ taps gets boringBigger coins plus +1 / +5 / −1 scoop buttons, with a big number counter
Grey "Step 1 of 3" lineEasy to miss what to do nextA guide character who says each step, and a highlighted step strip
Tries / wins / win-rate boxesGrown-up dashboard; "52%" means nothing to a young kidStars or pearls collected, and "You beat 82% of players" as a filling bar
Win = trophy + textFun, but quietBigger celebration: character dance, confetti, optional "ta-da" sound (off by default)
Lose = "So close!" textCan feel like failingFriendly retry ("Shark Man thinks you've got this!") and the same escalating hints
The puzzle rules, stats and "beat X%" backend stay exactly as they are. This is only a re-skin plus the friendlier controls above.

4. Recommendation

Pick A, Shark Man's Treasure Dive, with the kid-friendly fixes from section 1: read-aloud, +1/+5 scoops, bigger coins, pearls for wins, and a friendlier retry. It's the only style that turns the puzzle into a story a young kid can follow, and it makes Shark Man the face of both the paint kit and the game.
