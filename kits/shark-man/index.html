Questions from parents

What exactly do I get?

A PDF of printable line-art pages, sent to the email you enter at checkout. Print them at home on Letter or A4 paper.

How long until it arrives?

Within 24 hours. Dad sends every kit himself.

What paper and paints work best?

Thick paper (cardstock or watercolor paper) with watercolors. Regular printer paper is fine for markers and crayons.

How was the art made?

Dad draws the line art with AI from Ethan's ideas. Ethan does all the painting you see in the videos.
